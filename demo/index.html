          How Gina decides

          1. Usual rhythm: the median number of days between a customer's own orders.
          2. Missed: more than 1.5× their own rhythm since the last order. That starts the win-back journey.
          3. Went quiet: more than 3× their rhythm. Gina leaves them alone for 90 days.
          4. High value: top third in both frequency and spend, compared with the rest of this list. They get a person, not an email. With fewer than three customers, nobody is.
          5. One order only: under 30 days is New; after that, they didn't come back.
          6. Who is who: the customer ID when the list has one; otherwise the name, so two people with the same name count as one.
          7. Gina recommends the next step. Sending it, and checking consent and channel preferences, stays with you or your CRM.
